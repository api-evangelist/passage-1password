Zero-Shot Learning is a podcast about how AI is built, secured, and deployed. Hosted by Nancy Wang, 1Password CTO, and Dev Tagare, Senior Director of Engineering at Google Gemini, it offers a builder’s view of the architecture and complex decisions involved in shipping AI. In this episode, Rohan Varma, Product Lead for Codex at OpenAI, described what happens when teams move from using agents for one-off tasks to enabling autonomous coworkers.
