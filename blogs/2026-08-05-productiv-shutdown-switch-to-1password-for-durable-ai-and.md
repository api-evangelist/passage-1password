On August 2, 2026, Productiv told customers its SaaS management platform was shutting down on August 6, with account data deleted once access ended. Four days is not much time to pull years of app inventory, spend, and usage data out of a system you've come to depend on, especially with AI tools now adding a fast-moving new layer of spend and access to track on top of everything else. If you're facing that deadline, or just taking stock of what you'd do if your own platform disappeared tomorrow, here's why 1Password SaaS Manager is the strongest place to land.
