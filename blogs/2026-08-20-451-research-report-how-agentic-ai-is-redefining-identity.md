In the short time that AI agents have been a part of the enterprise, they have upended many of our bedrock assumptions about the nature of identity, access, development, and work itself. At 1Password, we’ve been in the trenches of the agentic revolution; we’ve seen its positive impact on productivity , and the serious concerns it raises about security . We’ve worked to build solutions that both harness AI’s potential and rein in its risks, and watched customers and colleagues grapple with the same issues.
