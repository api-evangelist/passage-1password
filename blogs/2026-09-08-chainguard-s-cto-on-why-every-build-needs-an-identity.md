How build identity and scoped access secure software Zero-Shot Learning is a podcast about how AI is built, secured, and deployed. Hosted by Nancy Wang, 1Password CTO, and Dev Tagare, Senior Director of Engineering at Google Gemini, it offers a builder’s view of the architecture and complex decisions involved in shipping AI. Matt Moore, co-founder and CTO of Chainguard , joined the podcast to discuss the overlap between open-source software supply chain security and identity and access controls.
