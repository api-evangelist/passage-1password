Zero-Shot Learning is a podcast about how AI is built, secured, and deployed. Hosted by Nancy Wang, 1Password CTO, and Dev Tagare, Senior Director of Engineering at Google Gemini, it offers a builder’s view of the architecture and complex decisions involved in shipping AI. Niantic Spatial’s CEO Inhi Cho Suh and Director of Product Management Eugene Chong joined Zero-Shot Learning to explore why spatial understanding is essential for AI systems operating in the physical world and how a grounding layer can turn spatial data into something models can use.
