The launch of 1Password Enterprise Password Manager – MSP Edition marked a critical step in 1Password’s mission to support our Managed Service Provider (MSP) partnerships. Now, we are pleased to announce that Advisory Solutions , a New York City-based MSP that works with companies worldwide, has reached the Certified Tier in the 1Password Partner program. 1Password's new Certified tier is a milestone we’ve implemented to recognize the investment and success of MSP partners who have reached 1,000 or more managed external users.
