Zero-Shot Learning is a podcast about how AI gets built, secured, and deployed. Hosted by Nancy Wang, 1Password CTO, and Dev Tagare, Senior Director of Engineering at Google, it’s a builder’s view of the architecture and the decisions it takes to ship with AI. Ankur Goyal, Founder and CEO of Braintrust , which bills itself as “the AI observability platform,” joined Zero-Shot Learning to talk about the problem every team shipping AI eventually faces, you can build something that works and then watch it quietly become something that doesn't.
